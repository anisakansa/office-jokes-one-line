# Office joke candidates

One line each, under 200 characters, original. Judged on: genuine joke,
surprise turn at the end, recognition of office life, tight wording, freshness.

1. "I put 'excellent communicator' on my CV, then spent four years replying to emails that just say 'per my last email.'"
2. "I joined the company for the free coffee; five years later I'm still here, and the coffee machine is still broken."
3. "Our open-plan office lets us collaborate freely — mostly on finding a quiet corner to hide from each other."
4. "My boss said his door is always open, so I asked for a raise; now the door is closed and so is my promotion."
5. "The printer finally works — it only took three IT tickets, two sacrifices, and one intern."

Picked: #1. The CV claim vs. the reality of "per my last email" is the sharpest
turn: the skill being advertised is exactly the thing the office grinds down,
and every office worker has both written and received that email.
